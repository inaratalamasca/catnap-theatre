# Ownership and licensing

Initial Catnap project organization: November 8, 2026.

First-party Catnap source is imported from the maintainer's existing local Catnap workspace; consult the source provenance notes for its original paths and versions. Copyright and other rights remain with their respective holders. No open-source license grant is made by this initial private repository. A maintainer licensing decision and review of imported assets/dependencies are required before wider redistribution.

Third-party dependencies retain their own licenses. AIU names/logos, community artwork, music and creator identities do not become Catnap-owned assets merely because a player displays them. Do not assume a private repository grants redistribution rights. No Kraesk/Nightwire or Rabbit/Luma private source is included.
